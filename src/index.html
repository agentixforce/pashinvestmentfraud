🚩 Red Flags — Real Estate Broker Fraud

Lessons from the PASH Investment Solution case

💰

Commission Sharing Promises

Beware of brokers promising to share their commission — the classic lure used by PASH Investment agents to attract victims.

📋

Terms Changed After Commitment

If terms change after payment — as PASH Investment did, jumping from 0.5% to 1% commission — walk away immediately.

📄

Suspicious Cheque Dates

Check all dates carefully. PASH Investment issued a cheque dated after its printing date — a deliberate defect to make it invalid.

📱

Screenshots as "Proof"

Screenshots are not valid proof. Always demand signed, stamped official documentation for every agreement.

🏃

Agents Disappear Post-Deal

Sanket Rustagi and Himanshu Rustagi of PASH Investment have disappeared and are unreachable after the fraud.

⚖️

No RERA Broker Registration

RERA has limited options for broker complaints. Always verify RERA registration before transacting with any Gurugram broker.

🏠 Home Buyers Alert — Gurgaon Real Estate Fraud

Gurugram has become a hotspot for broker fraud targeting NRI investors

URGENT: Protect yourself when dealing with Gurgaon real estate brokers. The PASH Investment Solution case is one of many documented fraud incidents in NCR. Know the patterns before you invest.

🚨 Common Fraud Patterns in Gurgaon

🎭

Commission Kickback Scams

Brokers promise to share commission, then disappear or provide worthless cheques — exactly what PASH Investment did.

📄

Fake Documentation

Forged signatures, impossible dates, invalid financial instruments used to avoid honouring agreements.

🔄

Bait and Switch

Attractive terms offered first, then changed after payment — pressuring buyers into unfavourable conditions.

🏢

Unregistered Firms

Firms operate without proper RERA licensing, using impressive office addresses to appear legitimate.

💸

Advance Fee Fraud

Demanding upfront payments for "exclusive deals" or "guaranteed approvals" that never materialise.

🌐

NRI Targeting

NRIs are specifically targeted because they cannot easily pursue legal action from abroad — a tactic used in the PASH Investment case.

🛡️ How to Protect Yourself

1

Verify Broker Credentials

  • Check RERA registration for agents and projects
  • Verify company registration with ROC
  • Ask for official business licences
  • Research online reviews and complaints
2

Document Everything

  • Keep written records of all promises
  • Record phone conversations (where legal)
  • Photograph documents before signing
  • Maintain full email/chat trails
3

Independent Verification

  • Visit property site independently
  • Verify directly with the builder
  • Get second opinions from other agents
  • Consult a real estate lawyer
4

Payment Safety

  • Never pay cash — use traceable transfers
  • Pay directly to builders, not brokers
  • Avoid advance fees for "exclusive deals"
  • Get receipts for every transaction

How can I report PASH Investment Solution or similar fraud? ▾
